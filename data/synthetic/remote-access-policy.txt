Title: Harbor Research Remote Access Policy
Document ID: HR-POL-001
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-10-01
Owner: Enterprise Security
Audience: All employees

Purpose
Define requirements for remote access to Harbor Research systems.

Access requirements
Employees must use an organization-managed device.
Multifactor authentication is required for remote access.
Access must use the approved enterprise remote access service.
Personal devices are not permitted to access internal systems.

Session requirements
Remote sessions must lock after 15 minutes of inactivity.
Users must reconnect and authenticate after a session expires.

Exceptions
Enterprise Security must approve exceptions before access begins.
Exceptions expire after 30 days unless explicitly renewed.

Reporting
Employees must report a lost managed device to the service desk
within one hour of discovering the loss.

Travel Requirements
Must get approval from supervisor to travel with equipment
Employees must use approved provided VPN
