Title: Harbor Research Acquisition Review Procedure
Document ID: HR-ACQ-001
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-10-01
Owner: Acquisition Management
Audience: Acquisition team

Purpose
Define review requirements for proposed technology purchases.

Required submission
The requester must provide a business justification,
estimated total cost, and proposed technical acceptance criteria.

Security review
Enterprise Security must review purchases that process
organizational data or connect to internal systems.
The review must occur before purchase approval.

Approval
The Acquisition Manager must approve purchases.
Requester approval alone is insufficient.

Acceptance
The technical owner must document whether the delivered
product meets the approved technical acceptance criteria.
Unresolved deficiencies must be recorded before acceptance.

Vendor Access
CIO Approves vendor access
Vendor Access expires at the end of the designated contract perior or one year whichever is sooner.
