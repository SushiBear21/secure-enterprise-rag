Title: Harbor Research Incident Response Runbook
Document ID: HR-SOC-001
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-10-01
Owner: Security Operations
Audience: SOC analysts

Purpose
Define initial handling of suspected endpoint compromise.

Initial triage
Create an incident record and assign an incident owner.
Record the affected device identifier and detection time.
Review endpoint alerts and available authentication logs.

Containment
The SOC incident lead must approve endpoint isolation.
Record the approval in the incident record before isolation.
If the incident lead is unavailable, escalate to the SOC manager.

Evidence handling
Store collected evidence in the approved incident evidence repository.
Record who collected each item and the collection time.
Do not upload evidence to personal accounts or public AI services.

Escalation
Notify the SOC manager within 15 minutes of confirming
a high-severity incident.

Incident closure
The Incident Manager must approve incident closure.
Documentation from every incident response phase must be
submitted to the approved incident evidence repository
before the incident can be closed.
